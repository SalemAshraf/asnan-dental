## Regulatory (official + secondary)
12. NPHIES Implementation Guide — https://portal.nphies.sa/ig/ + /ig/usecase-claims.html + /ig/ValueSet-dental-billing.html — official Saudi e-claims platform (FHIR). Claims: providers submit claims to insurers via NPHIES; dedicated Dental Billing value set (active 2024-12-24); employer-mandated insurance drives eClaims participation. Confidence: high (official).
13. Egypt PDPL Law 151/2020 (ILO NatLex PDF) — https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/111246/EGY111246%20Eng.pdf — law text. Confidence: high.
14. A&O Shearman — https://www.aoshearman.com/en/insights/ao-shearman-on-data/egypt-personal-data-protection-law-grace-period-ends-and-full-compliance-is-required — Egypt PDPL fully enforceable from 2026-11-01 after grace period expiry. Confidence: high (major firm).
15. Nilex Digital — https://nilexdigitalsystems.com/articles/health-data-protection-law-clinics-egypt — Egypt health data as sensitive data: explicit written consent, licence, necessity, per Al Tamimi summary. Confidence: medium (secondary).
16. NCC istitlaa Article 27 — https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/en/Transportation/NDMO/IMPLEMENTINGPDPL/Pages/Article_027.aspx — Saudi PDPL implementing regulation: controller must take organizational/technical/admin measures to safeguard health data. Confidence: high (official).
17. Bird & Bird — https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/saudi-arabia-health-data-under-the-personal-data-protection-law — extra conditions for health data under Saudi PDPL. Confidence: high.
18. iAssets/IntraZero — https://intrazero.com/en/solutions/saudi-health-data-residency-pdpl — vendor claim: KSA law does NOT mandate in-Kingdom hosting; transfer allowed under conditions; NCA removed hosting mandates 2024. Confidence: medium (vendor, contradicts common vendor marketing — flagged as contradiction).
19. Consenta — https://consentahealth.com/gcc-health-data-sovereignty — claims expectation of in-Kingdom health data hosting. Confidence: medium. Contradicts #18 — recorded.
20. Medoc HMS PDPL guide — https://medochms.com/resources/pdpl-healthcare-data-guide — PDPL applies to orgs processing data of individuals in KSA. Confidence: medium.
21. Medclinix (AR) — https://medclinix-rs.com/medical-system-hosting-saudi-arabia/ — transfer abroad not absolutely banned but restricted; in-Kingdom hosting is the simpler path. Confidence: medium.
