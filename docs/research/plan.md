# Research plan — Dental clinic SaaS, Egypt + Gulf

Date: 2026-11-22
Question: what does the dental clinic management software market look like in Egypt and the Gulf (KSA/UAE focus), who competes, what do they charge, which features are table stakes, and what regulatory constraints shape a first version?

Subquestions:
1. Who are the live competitors in Egypt? Gulf? (proof the demand exists + feature baseline)
2. Pricing models and price points (EGP, SAR, AED).
3. Payment/distribution behavior in Egypt (installments, WhatsApp, InstaPay).
4. Regulatory: Egypt PDPL 151/2020, Saudi PDPL + NPHIES + ZATCA, UAE health data rules.
5. Feature baseline for a dental-specific v1 (FDI chart, treatment plans, billing).

Method: web_search + web_fetch of vendor pages (primary), official regulatory sources (NPHIES IG, NCC istitlaa, law text), secondary legal analysis. Confidence labels per source. No market-size invention; only sourced numbers.
